Member sees

  • Next booking as the blue block — time, coach, spot number.
  • My week: booked, attended, cancelled-by-studio.
  • Spots open today with a Book action; already-booked cards go inert.
  • Never: occupancy %, other members' names, gym totals.

Staff sees

  • Attendance pending is the blue block — the one job waiting.
  • Today's sessions with booked / capacity, live and almost-full states.
  • Roster per session with Present / Absent toggles, plus cancel a session.
  • Never: week-long popularity, member counts, role management.

Admin sees

  • Avg occupancy is the blue block — the number the owner asks for.
  • Occupancy per class template, ranked most-booked, gym setup counts.
  • Entry points to trainers, class templates, users and roles.
  • Never: attendance toggles or booking on a member's behalf.

Chart rules

  • Donut = share of one whole (occupancy, present vs absent). Bars compare over time.
  • Max three slices per donut, ordered signal → warn → dim. More than three becomes a bar list.
  • The donut hole always carries the figure in mono, with a small caption under it.
  • Thin rings — this is a scoreboard, not a pie chart.
  • One highlighted bar per chart (the peak). The rest stay neutral.
  • Amber means near or over a limit, never just "second place".
  • Every chart keeps the raw count visible — a percentage alone is never enough.
  • Square corners, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no load animation.
