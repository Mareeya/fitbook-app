Rules to follow

  • Numbers are mono, everything else is Inter. Never mix inside one element.
  • Blue is functional: next-up block, live state, primary action. Nothing decorative.
  • One blue block per screen. Two competing blue blocks kills the effect.
  • Occupancy is a bar; the count under it stays small and dim.
  • Spots-left is the only occupancy figure allowed to be large, and only on detail.
  • State chips are solid fills, not outlines — except Open, which is an outline.
  • Square corners, 1px #DEDCD2 dividers, no shadows, no gradients.
  • Past classes drop to 34% opacity rather than changing colour.
